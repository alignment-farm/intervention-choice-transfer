## What would make the choice useful?

The initial hypothesis is conditional. Earlier outcomes can improve a decision
only when they supply relevant information beyond competent current observations.
Different action outcomes do not establish an advantage if one action always
dominates or a cheap visible rule already chooses correctly. A perfect chooser
also cannot repair an action set that leaves the complete task unresolved.

Changing the receiving model or interface may change which intervention is
useful. It need not make all old experience obsolete. Compare unchanged reuse
with selective observation or reacquisition when that distinction matters. Retain
the state and actual intervention behind each recorded outcome; an unexplained
success label is weak evidence about what will help a different recipient.

The root's existing AD1 and AD2 concern marginal intervention-choice value and
imperfect transfer across receiving histories. This study develops those questions
without assuming a learned controller is needed or prescribing where its policy
must persist. Complete later behavior and costs determine the useful boundary.

## Closest inspected public methods

- [MetaRSI, 2609.06396v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.06396v2), especially §§4.2–4.5
  and 5.4: an experience-revised improvement policy with a reported matched
  starting-system comparison. Generic learned allocation already has a direct
  public precedent. Root has not reproduced its outcomes. The inspected
  [RSI-Harness release](https://github.com/CosmosMind-ai/RSI-Harness/tree/737bf1f5a5a56f0c49bbd5180e71d651113038ac)
  supports configuration authoring but did not provide a verified reproduction
  of that policy-update experiment.
- [Qwen-Planner-Agent, 2609.29892v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.29892v1), §2.5
  and §3.2: prescribed model/harness alternation. Co-development gains alone
  cannot identify the contribution of learned intervention choice.
- [Ecdysis, 2609.11677v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.11677v2), §§3 and 6.2:
  aggregated failure review guides harness changes with a fixed task model.
  Recurrence does not establish a unique cause or a uniquely appropriate
  intervention substrate. Its
  [method-level release](https://github.com/cuiyu-ai/Ecdysis/tree/cf93866d545b0974dbb0bc83b39c31fbbdeeecb8)
  relies on external task collection, candidate editing and scoring callbacks.
- [ModularRSI, 2609.14857v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14857v1), §§3–6:
  module construction, integration and runtime composition. Harness transfer
  differs from transfer of experience about which intervention to choose.

These are selected methods readings and author-reported results, not verified
local experimental outcomes or an exhaustive novelty assessment.

The [dynamic composer](https://github.com/IQuestLab/ModularRSI/blob/b5c72c36b0d08ff93f00ee202a8fbdebe849dfb9/src/harbor/agents/terminus_2_modular/composer/llm_dynamic.py)

Those files do not supply a matched alternative-intervention dataset across
identified recipients. Do not infer the benefit of unchosen actions from a
successful final trajectory.

Public cases, exclusions and their explanations are development/background
knowledge after inspection. They cannot establish fresh confirmation of a claim
developed from them. The linked evolution dataset remains an uninspected lead;
root has not validated its tasks, labels or reference implementations. No public
code was installed or executed during preparation. Reuse is optional.

## Earlier local limits

These are historical root assessments, not authorizations to reopen their studies:

- [Remedy feasibility](https://github.com/alignment-farm/construct-2/blob/275de307ad4d61479df60dfee4c93246f54083b3/studies/2026-09-16-remedy-feasibility.md):
  the proposed S4 remedy distinction did not supply useful choice headroom;
  competent explicit access solved the candidate task.
- [Maintenance-decision transfer](https://github.com/alignment-farm/construct-2/blob/275de307ad4d61479df60dfee4c93246f54083b3/studies/2026-09-16-maintenance-transfer-findings.md):
  history-guided aggregate selection coexisted with incomplete available actions
  and weak observation controls. Better selection is insufficient by itself.
- [Migration and lineage](https://github.com/alignment-farm/construct-2/blob/275de307ad4d61479df60dfee4c93246f54083b3/studies/2026-09-17-migration-lineage-findings.md):
  unchanged guidance remained useful after migration while paid selection failed
  to repay its additional cost. Adaptation should not be presumed necessary.
