Begin the bounded intervention-choice-transfer investigation in this repository.

Read AGENTS.md, README.md and BACKGROUND.md. Use gpt-6-astra as investigator and
record your actual reasoning setting and provenance. Own the workload, methods,
diagnostic development, execution and publication.

Start by identifying a tractable task family, credible intervention alternatives
and one consequential receiving-agent or interface change. Establish whether
earlier intervention outcomes could improve a decision beyond competent current
diagnosis and unchanged reuse. The published assets are leads; their suitability,
implementation validity and complete-task consequences remain to be checked.

Separate learning better remedies from learning when to use them. An explicit
policy or small learned rule is eligible; no neural controller is required.
Develop functioning remedies and acquisition where useful, diagnose failed
learning, and preserve negative attempts. If a cheap ordinary rule explains the
comparison, publish that finding instead of manufacturing a selector advantage.

Use fresh material for claims developed during exploration. Measure complete
later work, preserved obligations and relevant acquisition, observation, use and
maintenance costs. Select a bounded informative comparison rather than a broad
co-evolution benchmark campaign.

Publish findings and evidence locally, keep README.md current, and explain the
next bounded step or reason to stop. Do not wait on the other active studies or
change their experiments. Root will assess publications while continuing its
independent research.
